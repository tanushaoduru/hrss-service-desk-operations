# Communication Templates (HRSS desk)

> Synthetic templates for practice. Keep messages short, polite, specific and free of unnecessary personal data. Use the requester's verified work email only. Replace `[ ]` placeholders. Never put salary or health details in the subject line.

## 1. Acknowledgement (all requests)
**Subject:** Your request [REQ-ID] - [request type]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for your request. I've logged it under reference **[REQ-ID]** and expect to complete it by **[due date]**. I'll update you if I need anything further.

Kind regards,
[Name] | HR Support Services

## 2. Request for more information
**Subject:** [REQ-ID] - information needed

Hi [First name],

To proceed with your [request type], could you please confirm: **[specific item, e.g. purpose of the salary certificate / delivery address]**? Your request will be completed within [x] working day(s) of receiving this.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## 3. Completion - reference letter
**Subject:** [REQ-ID] - your employment reference letter

Hi [First name],

Please find your reference letter attached (ref. [REF number]). It confirms your employment dates and role held. If any detail needs correcting, reply to this email and I'll reissue it promptly.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## 4. Completion - salary certificate
**Subject:** [REQ-ID] - your certificate (confidential)

Hi [First name],

Your certificate is attached (password sent separately / via secure channel). It has been issued for the purpose you stated: **[purpose]** and should only be used for that purpose.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## 5. Not eligible (decline politely, with reason and next step)
**Subject:** [REQ-ID] - update on your [scheme] request

Hi [First name],

Thank you for applying. Unfortunately I can't approve this request because **[specific reason, e.g. a previous claim falls within the minimum gap period]**. [Next step: you may be eligible again from [date] / please speak to your manager / contact [team] if you think this is incorrect.]

Kind regards,
[Name]

## 6. Delay / SLA at risk
**Subject:** [REQ-ID] - update on timing

Hi [First name],

I want to let you know your request is taking longer than expected because **[reason]**. The new expected completion date is **[date]**. I'm sorry for the delay and will update you as soon as it's done.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## 7. Coach change notifications
**To new coach / employee / previous coach (separate emails):**
"Please note that, effective [date], [Employee] will be coached by [New coach]. [Previous coach], thank you for your support to date. Please contact [team] with any questions."
*Do not state the reason for the change.*

## 8. Bike to Work - approved
"Your Bike to Work application [REQ-ID] has been approved for [item type], up to EUR [amount]. Next steps: [scheme provider process]. Please keep your proof of purchase."

## 9. Milestone message (adapt tone; check sensitivity first)
- **Baby:** "Congratulations on your new arrival! Wishing you and your family every happiness. Please enjoy this small gift from all of us."
- **Bereavement:** "Please accept our heartfelt condolences. We're thinking of you and your family at this difficult time." *(short, no marketing language)*
- **Long service:** "Thank you for [x] years of commitment - we truly appreciate everything you bring to the team."

## 10. Internal - suspected misdirected email / data incident
**To:** Team Lead; DPO / Privacy mailbox
**Subject:** URGENT - possible personal data incident [REQ-ID]

- What happened (one factual sentence):
- Date/time noticed:
- Data involved (type only - do not copy the data):
- Recipient(s) and whether recall was attempted:
- Actions taken so far:

*Report immediately. Do not delete the evidence. Do not email the data again.*
