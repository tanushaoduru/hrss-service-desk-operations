# Closing the Gaps - what this project can and cannot do

## What this project proves
Process understanding, SLA management, GDPR awareness, accuracy controls, documentation, communication and Excel skills for the six tasks in the job description.

## What it cannot prove (do not claim)
| Gap | Honest position |
|---|---|
| Workday experience | Conceptual/project exposure only - no live tenant |
| Professional HR experience | None from this project; you are a fresher |
| Multinational experience | Simulated ways of working (shift overlap, RACI, escalation, handover) - not real employment |

## Resume wording
**Do:**
- "HR Support Services desk simulation (Excel + SOPs): SLA tracking, document generation, eligibility validation, GDPR controls"
- "Workday HCM - conceptual/project exposure (worker data, effective dating, business-process concepts)"
- "Designed shift handover, escalation and four-eyes quality-check procedures for an offshore shared-services model (project)"

**Don't:**
- "Experienced in Workday"
- "X years of HR experience"
- Any employer logo or confidential material in the repo

## Genuine ways to narrow the gaps (do any that you can, then add to your CV only once done)
1. **GDPR:** complete a recognised introductory data-protection/GDPR course and list it only once finished.
2. **Workday:** use any legitimate learning access (college, training provider or employer programme). Do not list certifications you haven't earned.
3. **HR experience:** internships, graduate trainee programmes, HR volunteering for student/NGO bodies, shadowing someone in HR operations, or part-time admin roles handling confidential records.
4. **Excel:** practise lookups, dates, validation and dashboards; keep this workbook as evidence.
5. **Readiness:** be able to explain and demonstrate every sheet and SOP live.
